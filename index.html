About Me

I'm Ansh Verma, an Electronics & Communication Engineering student at VIT Chennai specializing in VLSI Design, RISC-V Architecture, AI/ML Hardware, and Embedded Systems.

My work lives at the gate level — I don't just study how processors work, I build them. From a fully verified single-cycle RV32I CPU in Verilog to hardware-accelerated CNNs on FPGA, I focus on the intersection of computer architecture and applied machine learning.

I believe the future of AI is in its silicon — efficient, purpose-built hardware designed to compute faster and smarter. That's the problem I'm working on.

RV32I RISC-V
Processor

A fully functional single-cycle RV32I processor implementing the base integer ISA from scratch in Verilog. Harvard architecture datapath with ALU, 32-register file, instruction & data memory, control unit, and immediate generator.
